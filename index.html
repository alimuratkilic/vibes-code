Care Routine

How I keep the racket playing like new.

Cleaning the rubbers

Spray cleaner, wipe gently from the center outward, and let them dry before casing.

Storing the racket

Always in a case, away from heat and direct sunlight, which dry out the rubber.

When to replace rubbers

Usually every 2–4 months of regular play, or when spin noticeably drops.
